What it means. Seven grey markers glide into a hairline target ring and turn green as they arrive, then the wordmark wipes in. They come "into range", which is the clinic's promise. The geometry is measured from OptimalLogo-Green.png and the wordmark is the site's own vector.
  • Use it on TV and screen ambient loops, reel outros, a site footer, and booking confirmations. Don't use it in a hero; the static logo belongs there.
  • Web: <optimal-logo variant="stage|inline" theme="light|dark" play="visible|load|manual">. Methods are play(), seek(s) and finish(). Under prefers-reduced-motion it always shows the finished logo.
