Is Optimal for me?

Optimal is probably for you if…

  • You've been looking for a family doctor for months or years and keep hitting dead ends.
  • You want more than 10 minutes with your provider.
  • You care about where your body is going in the next 20 years, not just this week.
  • You're navigating hormone changes and don't want to be dismissed.
  • You'd rather pay for primary care that works than hope the system gets better.

Optimal probably isn't for you if…

  • You're looking for a walk-in clinic (we're membership-based).
  • You need paediatric care (we see adults 18+).
  • You want full executive-health imaging like whole-body MRI (we refer out).
  • You're happy with your current family doctor — stay with them!
Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

Does this replace my family doctor?
Yes — you can transfer to us if you'd like, and we'll request records from your previous provider.
Is OHIP still billed for medical visits?
Core medical visits are still billed through OHIP. Membership covers non-OHIP services like unlimited messaging, InBody scans, the personalized health plan, and concierge coordination.
Can I add my spouse or family member?
Yes — household pricing available. Ask us about the rate during your Starter visit.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No long-term contract, plus our 90-day money-back guarantee.
How soon can I be seen after joining?
Our 3-day booking guarantee means you'll see your NP within 3 business days of joining — or your next month is free.
Do you see kids?
Optimal is an adults-only practice. We focus on primary care, preventive medicine, and hormone health for patients 18 and older.
What if I need imaging or a specialist?
We coordinate the referral and track the result. Your NP stays looped in.
How is Optimal different from Maple?
Maple is virtual and transactional. We're your in-person primary care provider who knows you over years.
How is Optimal different from Medcan or Cleveland Clinic?
A fraction of the price, built for Barrie, longevity-focused from day one. We're NP-led; they're physician-led — the care models are different, and the pricing reflects that.
